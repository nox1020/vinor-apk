## Title

Vinor — Property Partners

## Short description

Search listings, publish ads, manage a property, and track visits.

## Full description

Vinor is a property-partner app. Sign in with your mobile number, browse listings, publish an ad, manage your own property, and follow up on visits.

What you can do:

- Search Vinor listings and open a listing
- Save favorites
- My ads and property management
- Archive and restore a listing. It is not deleted; a closed badge keeps it off the active list
- Follow incoming and outgoing visits, with status and history, and archive that follow-up on its own
- Customers, wallet, and commission
- Invite a colleague
- Sales training, news, and notifications
- One-time SMS code sign-in. The app does not read your messages

Vinor has no in-app purchases. Words such as cash, negotiable, and exchange describe the property deal, not a payment to Vinor.
